Level calibration: not set (optional) Details & setup

Optional. Calibrate once per sitting: the result is kept on this device for 4 hours and reused for later screenings with the same device, headphones (Sennheiser HD 280 Pro recommended) and volume setting. Do not change the device volume after calibrating. Without calibration, tone levels rely on an unverified assumption about the device output.

Option 1: coupler or sound level meter

Place an HD 280 Pro earcup on the coupler (flat-plate adapter) and select Play coupler tone (a steady 1000 Hz tone in both earcups). Adjust the device volume until the meter reads 75 dB SPL, check the other earcup, then select Save.

Option 2: perceptual calibration (no meter)

Wear the headphones in a quiet room. First set the device volume so a steady tone is roughly comfortable. Then make four on-screen settings: the tone starts either too soft or too loud, and you select Louder or Softer until it is at your most comfortable listening level, neither soft nor loud. The starting level changes each time, so do not count button presses.

No calibration is set.

Room noise: off (optional) Details & setup

This is a rough estimate, not a calibrated sound level measurement or an OSHA compliance check. Your normal speaking voice is assumed to be 62.5 dBA (within 60-65 dBA). Keep the microphone in the same position after setting the reference, and use headphones during the hearing screening.

Before starting the hearing screener, read aloud at normal conversational volume for 8 seconds: "I am checking the sound around me so that I can use my hearing screener in a quiet place." Repeat if needed. Afterward, stop speaking.

Green means all four estimated octave-band levels are at or below their reference limits; red means at least one exceeds its limit. Grey means monitoring is off or no usable estimate is available. Limits are 47, 44, 54, and 59 dB SPL at 500, 1000, 2000, and 4000 Hz (OSHA-based limits adjusted for Sennheiser HD 280 Pro headphones).

Audio is processed locally and is not saved or uploaded. This optional indicator does not pause the hearing screener.

Microphone monitoring is off.
